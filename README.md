# 皮膚新編：轉錄與中英醫名對照

《皮膚新編》是嘉約翰口譯、林湘東筆述，1874年由廣州博濟醫局刊行的皮膚病漢文著作，現藏牛津 Bodleian Libraries，館藏號 Sinica 1790。英文導言以 Fenwick 的 Medical Diagnosis 後部為基礎，說明廣州用名、外國名稱音譯及跨地區術語徵集。原書分八冊、128節，附方、圖號及中英醫名對照，可供晚清醫學史、翻譯史與醫療傳教史查閱。本版在117個原印書面的固定初錄上，納入另行封存的獨立AI讀稿，再逐面進行非盲原圖裁定，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI、原表細格、註錨點及疑讀定位。保留原字、重複號與原空格，不含來源影像。未經人類全文雙校、專家審定或準確率量測。

Pi fu xin bian [Manual of skin diseases] was orally translated by John Glasgow Kerr and written down by Lin Xiangdong, and published by the Boji medical institution in Canton in 1874; this copy is Bodleian Sinica 1790. Its English notice identifies the latter part of Fenwick’s Medical Diagnosis as its basis and describes Canton names, transliteration and terminology collection. The work contains 128 sections in eight internal divisions, appended formulas, figure labels and bilingual names. Version 0.2.0 combines a fixed initial transcription of 117 original-print faces with a separately sealed independent AI reading, followed by full-face nonblind source-image adjudication. JSON, TXT and TEI retain source strings; table cells, original blanks and ditto marks, note anchors and uncertainty records support histories of medicine, translation and medical missions. Source images are excluded. No full human double collation, expert approval or accuracy measurement is claimed.

book.json 為主本，book.txt 與 book.tei.xml 保留相同單位字串。SOURCE-MAP.json 可回到官方原件；NOTE-ANCHORS.json 有 255 個文字插入點／接續定位；READING-MAP.json 與 TABLE-CELLS.csv 保留中英表細格。CHANGES.json 說明與固定 v0.1.0 的差異，表格表示轉換與原文改讀分開。
